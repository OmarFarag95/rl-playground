Reward formula

VariableMeaningBestAverage

Use + − * / ^, brackets, and abs, min, max, sqrt, exp, log, tanh, clip(x, lo, hi). The reward arrives once, at the end of each episode. Changing the formula keeps what the network has learned, so you can reshape behaviour while it trains.
